About the Laboratory

About

A discipline-category provincial key laboratory in Information, serving electronic information, advanced manufacturing, and the low-altitude economy.

The Key Laboratory of Real-virtual Integrated Autonomous Systems（RIAS） is hosted by the RUC Xiong'an Institute for Future Intelligent Manufacturing. Anchored in Xiong'an and linked with Beijing campuses, the lab addresses national priorities in AI, future industries, and intelligent unmanned systems, spanning agent systems, visual spatial computing, collaborative perception, and swarm decision-making along a sensing–cognition–decision–action pipeline. With partners from Renmin University of China and peer institutions, the lab is guided by a high-level academic committee.

Mission

  • Provincial key laboratory (discipline category)
  • Platform for real-virtual unmanned systems research
  • Demonstration hub for low-altitude & swarm applications
  • Open base for talent development

Research Directions

Five Directions

Five mutually reinforcing thrusts spanning real-virtual mapping, collaborative perception, multi-agent decision-making, cloud-brain platforms and testbeds, and cross-domain deployment.

  • 01
    Scene Reconstruction & Mapping
    Photorealistic indoor/outdoor reconstruction via 3D Gaussian Splatting, fusing geometry, physics, semantics, and texture for cross-domain real-virtual mapping and 4D dynamic virtual training fields.
  • 02
    Swarm Collaborative Perception
    Distributed feature extraction, lightweight models, and low-overhead exchange for multi-agent detection, tracking, localization, and situational awareness under weak communication and compute.
  • 03
    Multi-agent Decision & Optimization
    Distributed decision-making, task allocation, path planning, and collaborative optimization for heterogeneous unmanned systems in complex, high-interference environments.
  • 04
    Real-virtual Multi-agent Cloud-Brain Platform & Testbed
    A real-virtual integrated multi-agent cloud-brain platform and test system spanning sensing aggregation, collaborative assessment, decision scheduling, and closed-loop evaluation—for algorithm validation, system integration, and scenario rehearsal.
  • 05
    Cross-domain Deployment
    Ground–air, sea–air, and land–sea–air collaboration for public security, facility inspection, emergency rescue, and industrial logistics—from algorithms to field pilots.
