Academic Committee Members Mo Li and Zhen Shen Visit to Discuss Laboratory Development

On July 27, 2026, Academic Committee members Prof. Mo Li (The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology) and Prof. Zhen Shen (Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences) visited the Key Laboratory of Real-virtual Integrated Autonomous Systems（RIAS） and held in-depth discussions on laboratory development with Prof. Yongcai Wang, Associate Prof. Jinlong E, and colleagues.

The discussion covered laboratory planning, research directions, open collaboration, and talent cultivation. Both sides reached positive consensus on jointly advancing the key laboratory’s construction.

Prof. Mo Li is an IEEE Fellow and Director of the HKUST Low-Altitude Economy Research Center. Prof. Zhen Shen is a researcher at CASIA working on intelligent control and complex systems. Their visit as Academic Committee members provides important guidance for the laboratory’s development.
